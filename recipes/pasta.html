Description

This recipe will show you how to create cheesy garlic pasta.


Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Quarter cup grated cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta in a large pot of salted boiling water.
  2. In a large pan, melt butter over medium heat.
  3. Add the minced garlic.
  4. Add the cooked pasta to the pan.
  5. Toss the pasta to coat with garlic butter.
  6. Stir the cheese, salt, and pepper.
  7. Serve immediately.
