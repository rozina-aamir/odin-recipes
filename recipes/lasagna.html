Description

A lasagna is a delicious and filling pasta dish.

This recipe will teach you how to make a lasagna from scratch.


Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Lasagna Sheets
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the meat.
  2. Stir in the tomato sauce.
  3. Put one layer of meat in the pasta dish, then place lasagna sheets on top of the meat.
  4. Repeat this until the pasta dish is almost full.
  5. Cover the top lasagna sheets with cheese.
  6. Bake for 45 minutes.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
